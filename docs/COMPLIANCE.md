## The entrant must confirm (only you can)
- [ ] You are **18 or older**, which is the age of majority in India.
- [ ] You are **not** an employee, agent or immediate family or household member of Microsoft or the Premier League, and not a judge.
- [ ] You're entering **as an individual**. If you add teammates (up to 4), one Representative submits for the team.
- [ ] The Innovation Studio **Projects** page is fully filled in **before 2026-10-27, 11:59 pm PT**.

## Video rules (the most common way entries lose points or get rejected)
- [ ] **Shorter than 2:00.** Aim for 1:50; judges don't have to watch past 2:00.
- [ ] Shows the **live app actually working**, not slides only.
- [ ] Uploaded **Public or Unlisted** to YouTube, Vimeo, Facebook Video or Youku. The link must open without signing in.
- [ ] **No copyrighted music.** Use silence, your voice, or a track from the YouTube Audio Library.
- [ ] **No third-party trademarks on screen.** Before recording:
  - use a clean browser window with only the MatchMind, Fabric and GitHub tabs
  - hide the macOS Dock (⌥⌘D) and the bookmarks bar
  - close other apps' panels in VS Code
  - don't show real club, league or player names

  Microsoft and GitHub products are fine to show, because Microsoft is the sponsor.
- [ ] English narration, or English subtitles.

## After judging
Delete the Azure resources: `az group delete -n rg-matchmind --yes`. The Fabric trial ends on its own.
